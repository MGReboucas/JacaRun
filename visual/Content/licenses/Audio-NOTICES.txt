JacaRun audio

The six WAV effects in audio/ are original synthesized tones generated by
scripts/generate-sounds.ps1. They contain no third-party samples.

Audio playback uses the OpenAL Soft component supplied with Axmol 2.11.4,
commit b14941e6f50a0ce12489bd8f57041e093fb58819, in 3rdparty/openal.
OpenAL is distributed as a separate shared library (OpenAL32.dll / libopenal.so).
